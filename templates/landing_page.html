Rooted in the Community

FoodPrint is a food waste reporting system and data provider. Citizens report waste they find, the reports are stored and mapped, and environmental companies, organizations, and local governments use that data to decide where to act.

{# ============================================================ #} {# HOW IT WORKS — 3 real sequential steps #} {# ============================================================ #}

How it works

From sighting to cleanup

  1. Spot it

    Find food waste in your neighborhood, campus, or market.

  2. Report it

    Pin the location on the map, pick the waste category, and add a photo.

  3. Officials act

    Waste officials claim the report, collect the waste, and close the task.
