Problem

What does the world face?

While headlines often focus on plastic pollution, the data reveals a much larger hidden crisis: food waste is actually the single largest contributor to our landfills. Currently, food waste accounts for a staggering 40% of all municipal waste—double the volume of plastic. This means the biggest strain on our waste management systems, and the largest source of landfill methane emissions, comes directly from our kitchens and local services. We don't just have a trash problem; we have a food problem, and changing how we handle organic waste is the most impactful step we can take.

Rooted in the Community

FoodPrint is a food waste reporting system and data provider. Citizens report waste they find, the reports are stored and mapped, and environmental companies, organizations, and local governments use that data to decide where to act.
